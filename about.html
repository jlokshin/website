About

I'm a product leader focused on applied AI. At Capital One, I lead applied AI teams on the Enterprise Consumer Experiences team, working on how AI shows up in the products people use every day.

My path here is unusual. I started in history and international relations at USC, researched information warfare and national security policy at the American Enterprise Institute, and built AI and machine learning products in the Air Force and at SandboxAQ. I earned my MBA at Stanford.

I also edited Not One Inch, a book on NATO expansion published by Yale University Press, and I'm a member of the Council on Foreign Relations Young Professionals program. I live in Palo Alto.

This site collects my writing and projects. Views here are my own.
